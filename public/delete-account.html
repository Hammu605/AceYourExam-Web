Delete Account — AceYourExam

You can delete your AceYourExam account and associated Firebase data either directly inside the app or by submitting a request below.

90-Day Grace Period Policy: When you request account deletion, you are signed out immediately and your account is marked for deletion. If you do not log in again within 90 days, all your account data (profile, test history, streaks, and saved items) is permanently deleted from Firebase. If you log back in within 90 days, the deletion is automatically cancelled.

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Recommended)

  • Open AceYourExam on your phone.
  • Tap the You (Profile) tab at the bottom.
  • Tap Edit profile, scroll down, and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm your request — your account will be scheduled for 90-day permanent deletion.
