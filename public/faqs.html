Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I start a Mock Test?

Open the Home tab, choose your exam category (SSC, Railway, PSSSB, State Police, etc.), and tap any test card to begin.

2. Can I change my Target Exam or Study Medium?

Yes! Go to the You (Profile) tab and tap Goal or switch between Hinglish and English medium anytime.

3. How does Account Deletion work?

Go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. Your account is signed out immediately and permanently deleted from Firebase if you do not log in again for 90 days.

4. How can I contact support?

Use Help & Support inside the app or email us directly at xsupream@gmail.com.
