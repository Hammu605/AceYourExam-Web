Privacy Policy — AceYourExam

Last updated: November 25, 2026

AceYourExam ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information while you prepare for competitive exams.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Info: Name, email address (via Google Sign-In), username/handle, and target exam preference.
  • Study Activity: Mock test attempts, scores, study streaks, bookmarks, and focus timer sessions.
  • App Diagnostics: Basic crash and performance logs (via Firebase Crashlytics) to keep the app fast and bug-free.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Sync your test progress, bookmarks, and profile across devices.
  • Display study streaks, leaderboards, and personalized test recommendations.
  • Provide customer support and enforce account safety.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) with encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Account & Data Deletion (90-Day Policy)

You can delete your account anytime from inside the app (Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account) or via our Delete Account page. When you request deletion, your account is signed out immediately and scheduled for permanent deletion. If you do not log in again within 90 days, all your account data is permanently deleted from Firebase. Logging back in within 90 days automatically cancels the deletion request.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, contact us at xsupream@gmail.com.
